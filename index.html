Current Research

Riemannian Geometry

My current work in Riemannian geometry involved the study of manfiolds with metrics that admit strictly positive sectional curvature. Through the work of Eschenburg (1984) and Ziller (2018) I have been able to develop obstructions to strictly positve sectional curvature for certain Cheeger-deformed biquotient metrics. I continue to work on developing further examples of manifolds that might indeed admit strictly positive sectional curvature.

Quantum Field Theory

I am currently studying inequivalent Rindler vacua in Minkowski spacetime, focusing on Bogoliubov transformations between shifted Rindler frames and their implications for vacuum structure and thermodynamic interpretation. This work builds on Lochan and Padmanabhan's 2025 analysis of nested Rindler vacua and connects to broader questions in algebraic QFT, Green's functions, propagators, and quantum field theory in curved spacetime.

Selected Projects

A selection of projects spanning computational physics, machine learning, and algorithm engineering.

Balancing Inverted Single Pendulum with Reinforcement Learning

Designed a PPO-based reinforcement learning model using Schulman et al.'s Proximal Policy Optimization algorithm to balance single and double inverted pendulums in simulation, then built a physical single-pendulum prototype. Both the single- and double-pendulum models survived the full 1000 time steps for 10 out of 10 test runs.

Residual Neural Network Image Classifier

Implemented a residual neural network based on K. He, X. Zhang, S. Ren, and J. Sun, "Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition," arXiv:1512.03385, 2015. The model achieved 95% validation accuracy with 0.25 loss on bird species images.
